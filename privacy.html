Divido Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026 · Contact: dividosupport@gmail.com

Home · Terms

Divido (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the Divido mobile application.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we store profile details such as display name, email address, and optional avatar so we can identify you in expense groups.

Group and expense data. We store group names, expense titles, descriptions, amounts, currencies, split allocations, comments, receipts metadata, and settlement records that you and your group members create.

AI inputs. When you use Gemini-powered features (expense parsing, receipt scan, or AI group setup), prompt text, relevant member display names/emails, and receipt images are sent to Google’s Generative Language API through Divido’s authenticated backend solely to extract structured expense parameters. Divido does not use these requests to train global models. Free local parsing stays on your device.

Bank details. Sort codes and account numbers saved in Profile are stored only on your device in encrypted storage and are never uploaded to Divido servers.

Device tokens. If you enable push notifications we store a device push token to deliver group and settlement alerts.

2. How we use information

We use your data to operate bill splitting, settlements, invites, notifications, account security, abuse prevention, and customer support. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

3. Sharing

We share data with service providers that help us run Divido, including Supabase (authentication and database), Google (Generative AI for parsing features), and email delivery for invites/password reset. Group members you invite can see shared group expense data.

4. Security and retention

Data in transit is protected with TLS. You can request account deletion in the app; when deletion completes, associated account data is removed from our systems subject to short-lived backups and legal retention requirements.

5. Your choices

You may update profile details, disable notifications, remove bank details from the device, leave groups, or delete your account from Profile. Contact dividosupport@gmail.com for privacy requests.

6. Children

Divido is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of Divido after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
